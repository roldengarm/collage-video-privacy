# Snapbop – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

Snapbop ("the app", first released as "Collage Video") is made by Bobbyware. It turns photos you choose into short collage videos with music. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **your photos and videos never leave your device, and the only things the app sends us are a feedback message, if you choose to write one, and crash reports when something goes wrong.**

## What the app does not do

- It has no accounts, logins, or profiles.
- It has no advertising and no advertising ID.
- It has no analytics or tracking tools.
- It has no social features, chat, or public sharing inside the app.
- It does not send your photos or videos to us or to anyone else.

## Photos and videos

You pick photos with Android's own photo picker. The app only gets the photos you pick. All processing happens on your device. Finished videos are saved to your device only when you choose to save them.

If you tap **Share**, Android's normal share menu opens and you choose where the video goes. Whatever app you share to (for example a messaging app) handles the video under its own privacy policy.

## Feedback

Test versions of the app have a **Feedback** button. Using it is optional. If you fill in the form and tap **Send**, the app sends us:

- the face you picked and the topic you chose,
- the text you typed (up to 500 characters),
- the app's build number, the screen you were on, and the name of the video style you were using.

It does not send your photos, videos, name, account, location, or any device or advertising ID. Please do not write names or other personal details in the text box.

The message travels encrypted to a small service we run on Google Cloud, which passes it straight to a private issue list on GitHub that only the developer can read. The service does not store the message and does not pass on your IP address. Like any web service, Google Cloud keeps a short technical record of requests, which includes the IP address, for security; we do not use it to identify anyone, and it is deleted after 30 days. We use feedback only to fix and improve the app, we do not share it with anyone else, and we delete it once it has been dealt with. To have a message deleted sooner, contact us as described below.

## Crash reports

When the app crashes or something goes wrong, for example a video that fails
to save, the app sends a crash report to Google's Firebase Crashlytics service
so we can fix the problem. A report contains:

- what went wrong and where in the app's code it happened,
- a short record of what the app was doing just before (for example "saving
  a video, frame 120 of 240"),
- the phone's make and model, Android version, memory and free storage, the
  app's version, and the time,
- a random identifier that Crashlytics creates for this copy of the app. It
  is not linked to you, your Google account or any advertising ID.

Crash reports never contain your photos, videos, video titles, file names or
anything you type. We use them only to find and fix problems, and Google
processes them for us under the
[Firebase terms](https://firebase.google.com/terms/crashlytics) and keeps them
for 90 days. We do not use Firebase Analytics or any advertising service.

## Music

All music is included in the app. The app does not access your own music library.

## Children

The app is designed to be suitable for children. It never asks for personal information. The optional feedback form asks only for an opinion about the app, and tells users not to write their name or anything private. If a message does contain personal details, we delete it.

## Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about installs and device performance under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive personal information from that.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play page.
